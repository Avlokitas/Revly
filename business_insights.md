# Revly - Business Insights Report

## Dataset Overview

- Total Revenue: R$ 8.43M
- Total Orders: 42.9K
- Unique Customers: 42.1K
- Average Order Value (AOV): R$ 196.5
- Analysis Period: 2016-09-04 to 2018-09-03

---

# Key Business Insights

## 1. Geographic Revenue Leader

State **São Paulo** generated the highest revenue of **R$ 3211K**, making it the strongest regional market in the dataset.

### Business Impact

Revenue Concentration Insight
São Paulo (SP) generated the highest total revenue and had the largest customer base among all states, making it the most important market for overall business performance.

Customer Value Insight
Although SP generated the highest overall revenue, Paraíba (PB) recorded the highest average revenue per customer, indicating a smaller but more valuable customer segment with stronger spending behavior.

Business Recommendation
SP should remain the primary focus for customer acquisition and retention due to its scale, while PB presents an opportunity for premium offerings and targeted marketing campaigns aimed at high-value customers.

---

## 2. Revenue Concentration by Region

The top 5 revenue-generating states contributed **73.41%** of total revenue.

### Business Impact

Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of regions, suggesting opportunities for geographic expansion and market diversification.

The most frequently used payment method was **credit card**.

### Business Impact

## 7. High-Value Customer Contribution

## 8. Customer Retention Analysis

Repeat customers represented **3.03%** of the customer base.

### Business Impact

---

## 9. Seasonal Revenue Pattern

The month of **Nov 17** recorded the highest revenue of **R$ 1565721.4**.

### Business Impact

The business experiences seasonal demand patterns that can be leveraged for campaign planning and inventory management.

---

## 10. Average Customer Value

### Business Impact

# Strategic Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on top-performing states while exploring underpenetrated regions.
2. Invest in high-performing product categories to maximize revenue growth.
3. Improve customer retention programs to increase repeat purchase rates.
4. Monitor seasonal peaks and align inventory planning accordingly.
5. Continue optimizing preferred payment channels to reduce checkout friction.
